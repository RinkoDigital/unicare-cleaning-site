Frequently Asked Questions

Questions before joining the waitlist.

A clear overview of how Unicare Cleaning works and what makes the method different from traditional house cleaning.

What makes Unicare Cleaning different from traditional house cleaning services?

We follow a science-based and structured cleaning system designed to improve consistency, protect materials and support healthier home environments.

What is low-toxicity cleaning?

Whenever appropriate, we prioritize thoughtfully selected products that reduce unnecessary chemical exposure while maintaining effective cleaning performance.

Do you offer recurring cleaning?

Yes. We provide weekly, biweekly and monthly recurring residential cleaning services based on availability.

Do you bring your own cleaning products?

Yes. Professional equipment and carefully selected products are provided unless a client requests otherwise.

Who benefits most from the Unicare Method?

Our services are especially appreciated by families, busy professionals, households with children, pet owners and anyone seeking a more intentional approach to home care.
