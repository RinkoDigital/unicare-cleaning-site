Frequently asked questions

A few things to know before we begin.

Clear answers about the Unicare Method, service area and assessment process.

What makes Unicare Cleaning different from traditional house cleaning services?

We follow a structured, science-informed system designed to improve consistency, respect household routines, protect materials and support a healthier home environment.

What is low-toxicity cleaning?

Whenever appropriate, we prioritize thoughtfully selected products that reduce unnecessary chemical exposure while maintaining effective cleaning performance.

Do you offer recurring cleaning?

Yes. Weekly, biweekly and monthly recurring residential cleaning services are offered based on availability and the needs identified during the assessment process.

Do you bring your own cleaning products and equipment?

Yes. We bring professional equipment and carefully selected products unless a different arrangement is requested during your home assessment.

Which areas do you serve?

We serve selected homes in Bothell, Bellevue, Kirkland, Redmond, Woodinville, Kenmore and nearby communities across King and Snohomish counties.

What happens after I submit a Home Assessment?

We review the information, confirm whether your home is within our current service area and contact you about availability, recommendations and next steps. Submission does not confirm an appointment or final price.

Who benefits most from the Unicare Method?

Our service is especially appreciated by families, busy professionals, households with children, pet owners and anyone seeking a more intentional approach to home care.
